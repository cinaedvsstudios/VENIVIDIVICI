Sources actually consulted:

https://lexundria.com/suet_dom/15/r

Chapter 15 timed out on first direct fetch, but its full text was retrieved in search. Chapter 15 reports lightning strikes and a dream about Minerva; chapter 16 reports fruit offered on the day before his death and a leap from bed around midnight; chapter 17 specifies Stephanus's left arm wrapped in wool and a concealed dagger. These are ancient biographical reports, not independently established eyewitness details. Omens must not be presented as verified supernatural phenomena. Apples follow Rolfe's translation as requested; other translations differ on the fruit.
